EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Growth accelerated, operations released cash, and the next priority is converting momentum into liquidity.

December closed with record monthly sales of $2.02M, up 7.9% from November. After supplier purchases and a modeled operating-expense allowance, the business produced an estimated $385K operating surplus, equal to a 19.0% margin. Inventory fell by $377K and the cash conversion cycle shortened by 24.1 days, reducing cash tied up in operations by $238K.

The tradeoff is that customer balances rose to $2.81M and modeled cash coverage remains tight at 1.3×. Management’s January focus should be disciplined collections, continued inventory control, and protection of supplier terms.
